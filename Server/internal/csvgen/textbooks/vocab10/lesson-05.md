## Texts
### Text 1 — Education
Education plays a vital role in shaping an individual's future. A well-structured curriculum helps students improve their academic performance by providing them with the knowledge and skills needed for success. To evaluate students' progress, schools use different methods of assessment, such as exams, projects, and presentations. However, learning is not just about grades; extracurricular activities also contribute to personal growth by developing social skills and creativity.

To ensure educational equity, schools must provide access to quality educational resources for all students, regardless of their background. A student-centered approach allows learners to take an active role in their education, while differentiated instruction ensures that teaching methods meet individual needs. Modern teaching strategies, such as a blended learning approach, combine traditional classroom methods with digital tools, making education more flexible. Additionally, inquiry-based learning encourages critical thinking by allowing students to explore and question topics independently.

As students prepare for their future, they have different paths to choose from. Some pursue higher education, while others opt for vocational training to develop practical skills. For those unable to attend in person, distance learning provides valuable opportunities. Regardless of the path, lifelong learning is essential for personal and professional development. However, success in education also depends on honesty; maintaining academic integrity ensures that learning is fair and meaningful for everyone.
