## Texts
### Text 1 — Technology
Technology is advancing rapidly, shaping how we live, work, and communicate. Cutting-edge technology has led to digital transformation in many industries, from education and healthcare to business, transportation, and entertainment. For example, in business, automation has improved efficiency, reducing the need for manual labor, while in entertainment, streaming services have replaced traditional television, offering instant access to movies, shows, and music.

However, not everyone benefits equally from these advancements. The digital divide separates those with access to modern technology from those without, making it harder for some to develop digital literacy skills. In contrast, being tech-savvy is now essential in most jobs, as workers must understand digital tools, online security, and the importance of managing their digital footprint. Those who are computer-illiterate may struggle to adapt, limiting their opportunities in an increasingly digital world.

Although advanced technology gives rise to amazing inventions like augmented reality and autonomous vehicles, it also poses serious problems. Some people have misused technology to spread misinformation, invade privacy, commit cybercrimes, and exploit personal data for financial or political gain. Therefore, it is essential to establish strict regulations, promote responsible usage, and raise awareness about cybersecurity to minimize the adverse impacts of technological abuse on society.
