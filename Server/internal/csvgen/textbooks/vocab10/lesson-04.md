## Texts
### Text 1 — Employment
The labor market offers various job opportunities, ranging from temporary jobs to a permanent position. Some people work in white-collar professions, such as office jobs, while others have blue-collar roles that involve manual labor. Some prefer freelance work, which gives them flexibility, while others choose a nine-to-five job for stability.

In today's economy, to succeed in any job you choose, you must have the right qualifications and soft skills, such as communication and teamwork, as well as dedication. A strong work ethic, combined with continuous learning, increases the chances of building a stable and fulfilling career.

However, during an economic downturn, finding a job can be challenging. Businesses may freeze hiring, and some employees may be made redundant due to cost-cutting. However, when the economy improves, companies will focus on recruitment, seeking skilled workers to fill vacancies.

Ultimately, career decisions depend on not only the opportunities available but also personal goals. Ambitious employees work hard to earn a promotion, even when they are not entirely happy about their current job, while others, for whom job satisfaction is more important, may choose to resign.
