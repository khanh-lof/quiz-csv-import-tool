## Texts
### Text 1 — Culture
Culture is an essential part of a person's heritage, shaping their beliefs, values, and traditions. Culture goes beyond daily habits, encompassing rituals and ceremonies that celebrate important life events, such as weddings and religious festivals.

In a multicultural society, people from different backgrounds live together, bringing diversity in language, customs, and ways of life. While multiculturalism can lead to harmony and cross-cultural understanding, challenges may arise, such as discrimination against ethnic minority groups by the dominant group. This often happens due to stereotypes and misrepresentation, which create false ideas about certain cultures.

Maintaining one's cultural identity can be difficult in societies that encourage assimilation, where people are expected to adopt the norms of the majority, and where dominant groups enjoy certain privileges because of their social status. In these societies, minority groups often struggle to have their voices heard. Respecting and valuing different cultures can help build a more inclusive society, where everyone feels a sense of belonging. By promoting education and open-mindedness, we can reduce misunderstandings and create a world where cultural differences are appreciated rather than feared.
