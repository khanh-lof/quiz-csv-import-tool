## Texts
### Text 1 — The Environment
Environmental issues are becoming more serious, and urgent action is needed to protect our planet. Sustainable development is crucial to balancing economic growth with environmental protection, ensuring that future generations have the resources they need. However, one major challenge is our continued reliance on fossil fuels, which significantly increases our carbon footprint and contributes to global warming. To address this crisis, governments must shift towards renewable energy sources such as solar, wind, and hydropower, reducing dependence on non-renewable resources. In addition, effective pollution control and waste management strategies are essential to prevent further damage to ecosystems. Unfortunately, human activities such as illegal logging and habitat destruction threaten biodiversity, putting many species on the verge of extinction. At the same time, water scarcity is becoming a growing concern, especially in arid regions where access to clean water is limited. To combat these issues, authorities must enact laws that enforce stricter environmental regulations and promote environmental awareness among citizens. Businesses and individuals should also take measures to reduce waste, conserve water, and adopt sustainable practices. If we fail to act now, the well-being of our planet and future generations will be at stake, and the damage may become irreversible.
