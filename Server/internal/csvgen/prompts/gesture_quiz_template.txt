GESTURE QUIZ STAGES

This quiz is not imported into another platform: QuizTool plays it itself as a classroom game in which the learner answers with body gestures in front of a webcam. {{if .Single}}This quiz has a single stage, {{.StageList}}, so the learner plays only that gesture game and the stage fixes the shape of every row.{{else}}This quiz has {{.Count}} stages: {{.StageList}}. They are played in order from the easiest to the hardest, and a stage must be passed before the next one opens. Each stage is a different gesture game, so the stage of a row fixes both its difficulty and its shape.{{end}}

{{if .Skipped}}The number of a stage identifies its game, so it is not renumbered when the other stages are left out of this quiz. The Stage column of every row must be {{.Numbers}}, exactly as numbered above, and no row may use any other stage number.

{{end}}{{if .Single}}Every row belongs to that stage, so write each question to fit its difficulty and its format.{{else}}Classify every question by how difficult it really is for a learner of this lesson, and put it in the stage of this quiz that matches that difficulty and its format. Do not put a hard question in an early stage only because it has the right number of options.{{end}}

{{.Rules}}

{{if .OptionStages}}Options are shown in big type on a camera screen, so keep every option short: at most 8 Chinese characters or about 6 Vietnamese words in {{.OptionStages}}. The options of a row must all be different, exactly one of them is correct, and none carries a label such as "A.", "1." or "a)". Never write an option such as "Cả A và B" that refers to other options.

{{end}}Explanation: one short Vietnamese sentence shown after the learner answers, for example the tested word with its pinyin and meaning, or why the answer is right. Every row has one.

Stage: the number of the row's stage, which must be {{.Numbers}}, in the first column. No other stage number is allowed.

An empty column is still written as an empty field, so every row has exactly 7 fields.

{{if .Single}}Write exactly {{.Questions}} questions, all of stage {{.First}}, so {{.Total}} rows in total. Every one of them must be there, because the game needs them to decide whether the learner passed the stage.{{else}}Write exactly {{.Questions}} questions for every stage, {{.Total}} rows in total, and output the rows grouped by stage in ascending order of the stage number, stage {{.First}} first. Every stage must have all {{.Questions}} rows, because the game needs them to decide whether the learner passed the stage.{{end}}
