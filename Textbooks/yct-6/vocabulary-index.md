# YCT 6 标准教程 — vocabulary index

Transcribed **visually** from the printed 词语表 (book pp.120–124 = PDF pages 125–129).
Authoritative for pinyin and tone marks — do **not** take pinyin from
`textbook-fulltext-ocr.md`, whose OCR drops every diacritic.

Vietnamese glosses are added by this project (the book itself is Chinese + English
only). They are the meaning to put on a slide; the English is kept after the `/` so a
word can be traced back to the book's own gloss.

## How to read the table

- **★** = 超纲词, beyond the YCT 6 syllabus. Label these as optional / *mở rộng* on a
  slide, never as required vocabulary. 25 of the 195 words are ★.
- **课号** is the lesson. The printed 词语表 gives the *book page* where the word is
  introduced, not a lesson number; both columns are given here.
- 195 words total, 12–16 per lesson.
