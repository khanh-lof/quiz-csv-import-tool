## The 28 language points (Key Sentences)

YCT 6 has **no 语法 / 小语讲堂 section**. Each lesson's language points are the two
**Key Sentences** printed in the box on the lesson's first page — that is the book's
only explicit grammar statement, and it is what a rebuilt deck's grammar slides should
teach. Transcribed from each lesson's opening page.

### Lesson 1 (p.5)
- 猴子又聪明又可爱。— `又 A 又 B` "both … and …"
- 姐姐拿着一只小猴子。— `V + 着` (durative / state)

### Lesson 2 (p.13)
- 他数学好极了。— `Adj + 极了` "extremely"
- 他能讲明白。— resultative complement `V + 明白`

### Lesson 3 (p.21)
- 小鸟正在唱歌。— `正在 + V` (progressive)
- 兔子一边吃东西一边听小鸟唱歌。— `一边 A 一边 B` (simultaneous actions)

### Lesson 4 (p.29)
- 我还是打扫房间吧。— `还是 … 吧` "I'd rather …"
- 盘子洗干净了，房间也打扫干净了。— resultative complement `V + 干净`

### Lesson 5 (p.37)
- 句子写错了。— resultative complement `V + 错`
- 妈妈生气地说：“你太马虎了！” — adverbial particle `地`

### Lesson 6 (p.45)
- 大家都说我很漂亮，应该当演员。— `应该` / `当`
- 大家一看见我就叫我“电灯”。— `一 A 就 B` "as soon as"

### Lesson 7 (p.53)
- 他们俩虽然不是双胞胎，但是很像。— `虽然……但是……`
- 为了让大家知道他们不是双胞胎，张先生挂了一个牌子。— `为了` (purpose)

### Lesson 8 (p.61)
- 小鱼被小猫吃了。— `被` (passive)
- 我今天又被老师批评了。— `又` + 被 (repetition in the past)

### Lesson 9 (p.69)
- 我找得到。— potential complement `V + 得 + 结果`
- 如果找不到，我们给他打电话。— `如果……` + negative potential `V + 不 + 结果`

### Lesson 10 (p.77)
- 月亮离我们多远？— `离` (distance from)
- 上边什么都没有。— `什么都 + 否定` "nothing at all"

### Lesson 11 (p.85)
- 多便宜啊！— `多 + Adj + 啊！` (exclamation)
- 我花了两个小时就学会了。— `花 + 时间` + `就` (sooner than expected)

### Lesson 12 (p.93)
- 我记住了。— resultative complement `V + 住`
- 不要从高的地方向下跳。— `不要` + `从…向…`

### Lesson 13 (p.101)
- 我爱吃辣的，越辣越好。— `越 A 越 B`
- 如果不吃巧克力，我可能受不了。— `如果……` + `受不了`

### Lesson 14 (p.109)
- 我对这儿也不太熟悉。— `对……熟悉`
- 有的熊猫一直睡觉。— `有的……有的……` / `一直`

### Lesson 15
Review — no new Key Sentences.

## Standard lesson anatomy

## Recurring cast

YCT 6 has no fixed recurring cast the way HSK 2 does. Named characters appear
per-lesson only (小明, 小力, 小花, 张先生, 月月, 小海, 大力 …), most often in the
课文和小故事翻译 back matter. Don't assume a character carries across lessons —
check the lesson's own pages.
