## The 22 language points (Key Sentences)

YCT 1 has no 语法 / 小语讲堂 section. Each lesson's language points are the two **Key Sentences** printed in the
box on the lesson's first page.

### Lesson 1 (p.1)
- 你好！— Hello!
- 再见！— Goodbye!

### Lesson 2 (p.5)
- 你叫什么？— What's your name?
- 你认识她吗？— Do you know her? (`吗` question)

### Lesson 3 (p.9)
- 他是谁？— Who is he?
- 成龙是哪国人？— What is Jackie Chan's nationality?

### Lesson 4 (p.14)
- 你家有几口人？— How many people are there in your family?
- 你有姐姐吗？— Do you have big sisters?

### Lesson 5 (p.19)
- 你几岁？— How old are you?
- 你哥哥多大？— How old is your big brother?

### Lesson 6 (p.24)
- 妹妹的眼睛很小。— Little sister's eyes are small.
- 你的个子真高！— You're so tall!

### Lesson 7 (p.29)
- 这是谁的狗？— Whose dog is this?
- 这儿有很多小鱼。— There are lots of fish here.

### Lesson 8 (p.34)
- 你姐姐在家吗？— Is your big sister at home?
- 我去商店。— I'm going to the store.

### Lesson 9 (p.39)
- 你的生日是几月几号？— When is your birthday?
- 今天星期几？— What day is it today?

### Lesson 10 (p.44)
- 现在几点？— What time is it?
- 明天5点见。— Let's meet at 5 o'clock tomorrow.

### Lesson 11 (p.49)
- 你吃什么？— What would you like to eat?
- 我爱吃蛋糕。— I like to eat cakes.

### Lesson 12
Review — no new Key Sentences.

## Standard lesson anatomy

Key sentences box and Let's learn (new words) on the opening page, then Let's read (picture dialogues), Let's
match / Let's play / Let's sing or chant, and a Test page (Listening, Reading). Lessons 1–2 are four pages, 3–11 five.
