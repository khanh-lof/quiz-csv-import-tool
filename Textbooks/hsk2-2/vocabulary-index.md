# 《HSK标准教程2》 HSK Standard Course 2 — 词语表 / Vocabulary Index

Authoritative word list, transcribed visually from the textbook's back-matter 词语总表 (PDF pages 139–146, book pp. 121–128).

**How to read this list**

- 课号 = the lesson in which the word is introduced. Several numbers (e.g. `4, 8`) would mean the word appears in several lessons.
- **★ = 超纲词, beyond the HSK 2 syllabus** (the book prints `*`, in its own "Words Not Included in the Syllabus" table). 15 words: 班、度、粉色、更、公斤、过、欢迎、接、经常、米、拿、以后、一直、长 (zhǎng)、自行车. They are placed here in their letter section by pinyin; the book prints them in a separate table.
- Pinyin is exactly as printed, with tone marks (including `告诉 gàosu`, `右边 yòubian`, `左边 zuǒbian`).
- The book's table of words made of already-learned characters (旧字新词) is listed after the proper nouns, not in the main tables.

## Transcription notes

- The book prints, after 生词 (book pp. 121–125): 专有名词 (2 entries, p.126), 超纲词 (15 `*` words, pp. 126–127, with 级别 column) and 旧字新词 (10 words, p.127). 补充 Supplementary Vocabulary (p.128) has no lesson column and is not indexed. PDF p.147 is the colophon.
- The 15 ★ words are not in the printed 生词 table; they were merged into the letter sections by pinyin order, so their position is mine, not the book's. The caller's brief said 14; all 15 `*` rows are on pp. 126–127. ★长 (zhǎng, 13) is a different word from 长 (cháng, 4).
- Contents-page variants: 出院（出） (lesson 2), 粉色（粉） and 红色（红） (lesson 3). Here 出、粉色、红 are main entries; 出院 (2), 粉 (3), 红色 (3) are 旧字新词 rows. 帮、不错、外面、手、路口 (lessons 4, 5, 5, 13, 13), 新年 (15) and 有意思 (14) are listed on the contents pages as lesson words and are 旧字新词 rows, as are 出院、粉、红色.
- Page 143 of the scan is skewed, so lesson numbers sit between rows; lessons were checked against the contents pages. 意思 reads as 5 in the table but the contents list it in lesson 14 (and 有意思 is lesson 14): lesson 14 is used. 丈夫 (number cut off in the scan), 跑步, 旁边, 件 and 晚上 (numbers under the watermark) take the lesson the contents pages give: 3, 2, 3, 5, 4.
- Contents-page cross-check: every lesson's 词汇 list agrees with the 课号 transcribed (including ★ and 旧字新词 words).
