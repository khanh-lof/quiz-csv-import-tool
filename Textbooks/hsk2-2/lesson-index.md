# 《HSK标准教程2》 HSK Standard Course 2 — Lesson & Language-Point Index

Authoritative index, transcribed visually from the official textbook's 目录 / Contents
(`HSK Standard Course Level 2.pdf`, PDF pp.10–14).

**Book facts (from 本册说明, book front matter):**

- Level: **HSK 2.0 — Level 2 (二级)**, 北京语言大学出版社 / 汉考国际 (CTI), chief editor 姜丽萍, 2014.
- Audience: learners who have studied 30–34 class hours and know about 150 words, preparing for the HSK (Level 2) exam.
- **15 lessons.** Each lesson has one theme in four scenes (场景) of two turns each, 10–15 new words and 2–4 language points. The syllabus is the 300 words of HSK 2.0 Level 2, including the 150 of Level 1.
- Only **14 words are beyond the syllabus** (超纲词, printed with `*`; nearly all are Level 3 words). They are marked **★** in the vocabulary index.
- A 文化 page follows lessons 5, 10 and 15: Chinese table manners, Chinese tea culture, the Chinese New Year (Spring Festival).

**Standard lesson structure:** 热身 (warm-up), 课文 (four scenes with 生词 beside each), 注释 (language-point notes), 练习, 语音 (pronunciation), 汉字 (characters), 运用 (activities).

**Page-number mapping:** PDF page number = book page number + 16 up to lesson 9's text pages, and book page + 18 from lesson 10 on (two extra pages fall inside lesson 9's pages). Book p.1 = PDF p.17; 词语总表 is book p.121 = PDF p.139; 补充词语 is PDF p.146.

## Language points (语法 / Grammar)

### Lesson 1 — 九月去北京旅游最好

1. 助动词“要” — The Auxiliary Verb “要”
2. 程度副词“最” — The Adverb of Degree “最”
3. 概数的表达：几、多 — “几” and “多”: expressions of approximate numbers

### Lesson 2 — 我每天六点起床

1. 用“是不是”的问句 — Questions Using “是不是”
2. 代词“每” — The Pronoun “每”
3. 疑问代词“多” — The Interrogative Pronoun “多”

### Lesson 3 — 左边那个红色的是我的

1. “的”字短语 — The “的” Phrase
2. 一下 — The Numeral Classifier “一下”
3. 语气副词“真” — The Modal Adverb “真”

### Lesson 4 — 这个工作是他帮我介绍的

1. “是……的”句：强调施事 — The Structure “是……的”: used to emphasize the agent of an action
2. 表示时间：……的时候 — “……的时候” Indicating Time
3. 时间副词“已经” — The Adverb of Time “已经”

### Lesson 5 — 就买这件吧

1. 副词“就” — The Adverb “就”
2. 语气副词“还”（1） — The Modal Adverb “还” (1)
3. 程度副词“有点儿” — The Adverbial Modifier “有点儿”

### Lesson 6 — 你怎么不吃了

1. 疑问代词“怎么” — The Interrogative Pronoun “怎么”
2. 量词的重叠 — Reduplication of Measure Words
3. 关联词“因为……，所以……” — The Pair of Conjunctions “因为……，所以……”

### Lesson 7 — 你家离公司远吗

1. 语气副词“还”（2） — The Modal Adverb “还” (2)
2. 时间副词“就” — The Adverb of Time “就”
3. 离 — The Verb “离”
4. 语气助词“呢” — The Modal Particle “呢”

### Lesson 8 — 让我想想再告诉你

1. 疑问句“……，好吗” — The Interrogative Sentence “……，好吗”
2. 副词“再” — The Adverb “再”
3. 兼语句 — Pivotal Sentences
4. 动词的重叠 — Reduplication of Verbs

### Lesson 9 — 题太多，我没做完

1. 结果补语 — Complements of Result
2. 介词“从” — The Preposition “从”
3. “第~”表示顺序 — “第~” Indicating Order

### Lesson 10 — 别找了，手机在桌子上呢

1. 祈使句：不要……了；别……了 — The Imperative Sentence “不要……了/别……了”
2. 介词“对” — The Preposition “对”

### Lesson 11 — 他比我大三岁

1. 动词结构做定语 — A Verb (Phrase) Used as an Attributive Modifier
2. “比”字句（1） — The “比” Sentence (1)
3. 助动词“可能” — The Auxiliary Verb “可能”

### Lesson 12 — 你穿得太少了

1. 状态补语 — Complements of State
2. “比”字句（2） — The “比” Sentence (2)

### Lesson 13 — 门开着呢

1. 动态助词“着” — The Aspect Particle “着”
2. 反问句“不是……吗” — The Rhetorical Question “不是……吗”
3. 介词“往” — The Preposition “往”

### Lesson 14 — 你看过那个电影吗

1. 动态助词“过” — The Aspect Particle “过”
2. 关联词“虽然……，但是……” — The Pair of Conjunctions “虽然……，但是……”
3. 动量补语“次” — The Complement of Frequency “次”

### Lesson 15 — 新年就要到了

1. 动作的状态：“要……了” — “要……了” Indicating the State of an Action
2. “都……了” — The Structure “都……了”
