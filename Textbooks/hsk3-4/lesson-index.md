## Book facts

- **《新HSK教程4》** (外语教学与研究出版社 / FLTRP, 2025 — the **new HSK 3.0 series**, *not* 《HSK标准教程》).
  For learners with 114–152 class hours and ~1,000 words behind them, preparing for new HSK 4.
- **2 volumes × 10 lessons, numbered continuously**: 4上 = lessons 1–10, 4下 = lessons 11–20.
  **80 language points** (4 per lesson). 80–120 class hours, 4–5 per lesson.
- Two PDFs in `book/`, each image-only (no text layer):

## Standard lesson structure (from 使用说明 + Lesson 1)

| Section | 中文 | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Objectives | 目标 | 3 checkbox objectives |
| Warm-Up | 热身 | (1) match 6 words to 6 photos (pinyin under each word), (2) pair Q&A, 2 questions |
| Texts | 课文 1–4 | **课文1 and 课文2 = dialogues** (听对话，选择正确答案 ×2 → dialogue → 分角色朗读 → 根据课文内容回答问题 → 根据提示复述课文); **课文3 and 课文4 = narrative passages** with their own title (课文3: 听课文，选择 ×2; 课文4: 快速阅读短文，选择 ×2) → 大声朗读 → 回答问题 → 复述 |
| New Words | 生词 | one box per text, numbered continuously ①…㊵ across the lesson, with pinyin, POS and English; ~40 words per lesson |
| Xiaoyu's Classroom | 小语讲堂 | **4 per lesson, one after each text** (not 3 as in HSK 3): explanation + 3 examples + a 完成对话 or 完成句子 drill (3 items) |
| Comprehensive Exercises | 综合练习 | 选词填空 (10 items, two 5-word banks A–E: 5 sentences + 5 mini-dialogues), 选择合适的内容补充短文 (4 options A–D into a passage), 看图，用词造句 (3 photos + given words) |
| Task-Based Activities | 任务活动 | two parts: oral (角色扮演 / 小组讨论 / 辩论 / 调查) and 写作 (a short passage, "不少于120字" in 4上, 140字 in 4下) |
| Xiaoyu's Bonus | 小语的彩蛋 | culture video note; **14 in the whole set** — lessons 4–10, 12, 15–20 |
| Learning Summary | 学习小结 | **every lesson**: a self-assessment can-do table (没问题 / 还可以 / 需要努力) |
| Xiaoyu's Tip | 小语助力 | short inline glosses beside a text |

Audio is numbered `N-1 … N-8` per lesson in the book (texts and their 生词 boxes alternate:
N-1 text 1, N-2 words 1, …, N-7 text 4, N-8 words 4); the bonus video is `N-1` of its own series.
