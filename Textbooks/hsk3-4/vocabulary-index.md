# HSK 4 vocabulary index (词语表)

Transcribed visually from the printed 词语表 of both volumes (300 dpi crops, every pinyin
syllable read from the page, then cross-checked against `pypinyin`; every disagreement was
a deliberate book spelling — neutral tones, 一/不 tone sandhi — and is kept as printed).
**★ = 超纲词** (the book: "前面加 * 的是本级超纲词" — beyond this level's syllabus).

- **The 词语表 has no POS column.** POS is printed only in each lesson's 生词 box —
  read it from the lesson's 生词 box on the page images (`pages/`) and check the abbreviation against the table below.
- Lessons are numbered continuously across the two volumes: 4上 = 1–10, 4下 = 11–20. Each
  volume prints its own A–Z list; they are kept separate below, in the book's own order.
- Apostrophes are normalised to a straight `'` (the book prints ’): dá'àn, ǒu'ěr.
- Pinyin kept exactly as printed, including: 一/不 sandhi (búguò, yíxià, yìshēng); neutral
  tones (hùshi, shōushi, zhīshi, bùfen, chuānghu); 值得 zhídé; 种 zhòng (L2, the verb);
  嗯 ǹg; 熟 shú/shóu; 费用 fèiyong (sic); 父子 fùzǐ, 母子 mǔzǐ, 孔子 Kǒngzǐ.
- **One correction:** 辣子鸡 prints as "Làzljī" (the i's dot is lost in the print);
  recorded as the standard **Làzijī**.
- 3D打印机 / AI眼镜 (L18) are printed with a space after the Latin prefix.
