# 《新HSK教程1》 New HSK Course 1 — 词语表 / Vocabulary Index

Authoritative word list, transcribed visually from the official textbook's back-matter
词语表 (`Gtrinh HSK 1 - Ms.Ha Chinese.pdf`, PDF pages 136–142; book pp. 122–128).

**How to read this list**

- 课号 = the lesson in which the word is introduced. Several numbers (e.g. `4, 8`) means the word appears in several lessons, usually with different senses or uses.
- **★ = 超纲词, beyond the HSK 3.0 Level-1 syllabus** (the book prints `*`). 8 words: 病人、服务员、机场、接、斤、售货员、药、医. On slides they must be marked optional/mở rộng — never presented as required vocabulary.
- 307 entries in total: 299 syllabus words + 8 ★, plus 12 专有名词.
- Order and pinyin are exactly as printed, including the book's choices `边 bian/biān`, `里 li/lǐ`, `上 shang/shàng`, `谁 shéi/shuí`, `喝 hē`, `小朋友 xiǎopéngyǒu`, capitalised `Xīngqīrì` / `Xīngqītiān`.
- HSK 1 is the first level: there is no lower level to inherit from. "Already met" means only this list up to the current lesson.
- Cross-check done at scan time: 184 of the 307 words were matched in the lessons' own 生词 boxes (OCR), all with the lesson number given here.
