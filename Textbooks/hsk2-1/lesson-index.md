**Book facts (from 本册说明, book p.6):**

- Level: **HSK 2.0 — Level 1 (一级)**, 北京语言大学出版社 / 汉考国际 (CTI), chief editor 姜丽萍, 2013.
- Audience: complete beginners, and learners preparing for the HSK (Level 1) exam.
- **15 lessons.** Lessons 1 and 2 are mainly pronunciation. From lesson 3 each lesson has one theme in three scenes (场景), 10–15 new words and 3–4 language-point notes (注释).
- The vocabulary follows the **150 words** of the HSK 2.0 Level 1 syllabus. Only 10 words are beyond it (超纲词, printed with `*`; all of them are Level 2 or 3 words): 您、口、好吃、问、身体、也、给、吧、啊、一起.
- Words marked **★** in the vocabulary index are these beyond-syllabus words.

**Standard lesson structure:** 课文 (three numbered scenes with 生词 beside each), 注释 (language-point notes, lessons 3–15), 语音 / 拼音 (pronunciation, lessons 1–15), 汉字 (characters), 练习 (exercises). A 文化 (culture) page follows lessons 5, 10 and 15.

## Language points (注释 / Notes)

### Lesson 1 — 你好

No 注释. Greetings 你好 / 您好 / 你们好 and 对不起 / 没关系. Pronunciation: initials and finals (1), the four tones, syllables, tone sandhi 3rd + 3rd.

### Lesson 2 — 谢谢你

No 注释. 谢谢 / 不客气 / 再见. Pronunciation: initials and finals (2), the neutral tone, pinyin rules (1).

### Lesson 3 — 你叫什么名字

1. 疑问代词“什么” — The Interrogative Pronoun “什么”
2. “是”字句 — The “是” Sentence
3. 用“吗”的疑问句 — Interrogative Sentences with “吗”

### Lesson 4 — 她是我的汉语老师

1. 疑问代词“谁”、“哪” — The Interrogative Pronouns “谁” and “哪”
2. 结构助词“的” — The Structural Particle “的”
3. 疑问助词“呢”（1） — The Interrogative Particle “呢” (1)

### Lesson 5 — 她女儿今年二十岁

1. 疑问代词“几” — The Interrogative Pronoun “几”
2. 百以内的数字 — Numbers below 100
3. “了”表变化 — “了” Indicating a Change
4. “多 + 大”表示疑问 — The Interrogative Phrase “多 + 大”

文化: 中国人对年龄的询问方法 — Ways of Asking a Chinese Person's Age (book p.39)

### Lesson 6 — 我会说汉语

1. 能愿动词“会”（1） — The Modal Verb “会” (1)
2. 形容词谓语句 — Sentences with an Adjectival Predicate
3. 疑问代词“怎么”（1） — The Interrogative Pronoun “怎么” (1)

### Lesson 7 — 今天几号

1. 日期的表达（1）：月、日 / 号、星期 — Expression of a Date (1): month, date, day of the week
2. 名词谓语句 — Sentences with a Nominal Predicate
3. 连动句（1）：去 + 地方 + 做什么 — Sentences with a Serial Verb Construction (1): 去 + place + to do sth.

### Lesson 8 — 我想喝茶

### Lesson 9 — 你儿子在哪儿工作

4. 疑问助词“呢”（2） — The Interrogative Particle “呢” (2)
