# 《HSK标准教程1》 HSK Standard Course 1 — 词语表 / Vocabulary Index

Authoritative word list, transcribed visually from the textbook's back-matter 词语总表 (PDF pages 133–141, book pp. 120–128).

**How to read this list**

- 课号 = the lesson in which the word is introduced. `5 (notes)` in the book (the numerals 一 to 十) is written `5`: they are introduced in that lesson's notes. Several numbers (e.g. `4, 8`) would mean the word appears in several lessons.
- **★ = 超纲词, beyond the HSK 1 syllabus** (the book prints `*`, in its own "Words Not Included in the Syllabus" table). 10 words: 啊、吧、给、好吃、口、您、身体、问、也、一起. They are placed here in their letter section by pinyin; the book prints them in a separate table.
- Pinyin is exactly as printed, with tone marks (including `里 li`, `上 shang`, `谁 shéi`, `喝 hē`).
- The book's two further tables of words made of already-learned characters (旧字新词) are listed after the proper nouns, not in the main tables.

## Transcription notes

- The book prints three sections after 生词: 专有名词 (8 entries, found on book p.125 / PDF p.138), 超纲词 (10 `*` words, book pp.125–126, with 级别 column 二级/三级) and 旧字新词 (21 words, book pp.126–127). 汉字总表 starts on book p.128 and is not indexed; PDF p.141 is the colophon.
- The 10 ★ words are not in the printed 生词 table; they were merged into the letter sections by pinyin order, so their position is mine, not the book's.
- The 旧字新词 words are included in the lessons' 词汇 on the contents pages (e.g. 今年 in lesson 5, 吃饭 in 11) but are not in the 生词 table; they sit in their own section here. Merge them into the main tables if lesson vocabulary should include them.
- Contents-page variants: 下面（下） (lesson 9), 太（太……了） and 下雨（下、雨） (lesson 12), 学习（学） (lesson 13), 少（不少） (lesson 14), 没有（没） (lesson 10). Here 下面、下、太、学习、少、没有 are main entries; 下面 (9), 太……了 (12), 下 (12, rain), 学 (13), 不少 (14), 没 (10), 雨 (12) are 旧字新词 rows. Main-table 下 is lesson 9 (below), 旧字新词 下 is lesson 12 (to fall): the same character twice.
- Not read as rows: the 补充 Supplementary Vocabulary tables (book p.127, no lesson column).
- Watermark partly covers a few digits: 太 (12), 年 (15), 女儿 (5), 回来 (14). All agree with the contents pages.
- Contents-page cross-check: every lesson's 词汇 list agrees with the 课号 transcribed (including ★ and 旧字新词 words). The numerals 一 to 十 are not listed on the contents pages (lesson 5 notes) but are in the 生词 table as `5 (notes)`.
