# 《新HSK教程2》 New HSK Course 2 — Lesson & Language-Point Index

Authoritative index, transcribed visually from the official textbook's 目录 / CONTENTS
(`Gtrinh HSK 2 - Ms.Ha Chinese.pdf`, PDF pages 13–15).

**Book facts (from 使用说明 / Instructions for Use, book p. V):**

- Level: **HSK 3.0 — Level 2 (二级)**
- Audience: learners who have completed ~30–36 class hours and mastered ~300 words, preparing for the HSK (Level 2) exam.
- **15 lessons**, vocabulary aligned to the **200 words** required by the new HSK syllabus (with slight extension), **45 language points** (3 per lesson).
- Recommended teaching time: 30–45 class hours total, 2–3 class hours per lesson.
- Words marked **★** in the vocabulary index are **beyond the syllabus for this level** (超纲词) — treat them as optional enrichment, never as required Level-2 vocabulary.

**Standard lesson structure (课文板块):**

| Section | Chinese | What it contains |
|---|---|---|
| Objectives | 目标 | The teaching/learning focus of the lesson |

| Texts | 课文 | **Four texts per lesson.** Texts 1–3 are situational dialogues; Text 4 is a narrative/monologue |
| New Words | 生词 | Vocabulary box after each text |

## The 45 language points (小语讲堂 / Xiaoyu's Classroom)

### Lesson 1 — 她请我们吃了北京烤鸭

1. 语气助词“吧”（2） — Modal Particle “吧” (2)
2. “是……的”句 — “是……的” Sentence
3. 兼语句 — Pivotal Sentences

### Lesson 2 — 还是打车去北大吧

1. 固定格式“还是……吧” — Fixed Pattern “还是……吧”
2. 用“多”表达概数 — Expressing Approximate Numbers with “多”
3. 动词或动词性短语、主谓短语作定语 — Verbs, Verbal Phrases, and Subject-Predicate Phrases as Attributives

### Lesson 3 — 我想去西安旅游

1. 结果补语 — Complement of Result
2. 动词重叠（1） — Verb Reduplication (1)
3. 动词重叠（2） — Verb Reduplication (2)

### Lesson 4 — 你穿红色的很好看

1. 动态助词“过” — Aspect Particle “过”
2. 因果复句“因为……，所以……” — Causal Complex Sentence “因为……，所以……”
3. “的”字短语 — “的” Phrase

### Lesson 5 — 第一次去中国朋友家

1. 简单趋向补语（1） — Simple Complement of Direction (1)
2. 简单趋向补语（2） — Simple Complement of Direction (2)
3. 固定格式“都……了” — Fixed Pattern “都……了”

### Lesson 6 — 小雪，生日快乐！

1. 形容词重叠 — Adjective Reduplication
2. 固定短语“什么的” — Set Phrase “什么的”
3. 结构助词“地” — Structural Particle “地”

### Lesson 7 — 他篮球打得很好

1. 紧缩复句“一……就……” — Contracted Complex Sentence “一……就……”
2. 状态补语（1） — Complement of State (1)
3. 状态补语（2） — Complement of State (2)

### Lesson 8 — 虽然你忘了，但是我记得

1. 比较句（1） — Comparative Sentences (1)
2. 比较句（2） — Comparative Sentences (2)
3. 转折复句“虽然……，但是……” — Adversative Complex Sentence “虽然……，但是……”

### Lesson 9 — 我去买杯奶茶

1. 比较句（3） — Comparative Sentences (3)
2. 动词“离” — Verb “离”
3. 时量补语（1） — Complement of Duration (1)

### Lesson 10 — 就要考试了

1. 主谓谓语句 — Sentences with a Subject-Predicate Phrase as the Predicate
2. 选择问句 — Alternative Questions
3. 固定格式“要/快/快要/就要……了” — Fixed Pattern “要/快/快要/就要……了”

### Lesson 11 — 我最喜欢吃中国菜

1. 动态助词“着”（1） — Aspect Particle “着” (1)
2. 动态助词“着”（2） — Aspect Particle “着” (2)
3. 程度副词“最” — Adverb of Degree “最”

### Lesson 12 — 这里比北京冷多了

1. 比较句（4） — Comparative Sentences (4)
2. 比较句（5） — Comparative Sentences (5)
3. 比较句（6） — Comparative Sentences (6)

### Lesson 13 — 我们爱上中文课

1. 双宾语句（2） — Double-Object Sentences (2)
2. 比较句（7） — Comparative Sentences (7)
3. 比较句（8） — Comparative Sentences (8)

### Lesson 14 — 一个人过年多没意思啊

1. 存现句（2） — Existential Sentences (2)
2. 程度副词“多” — Adverb of Degree “多”
3. 复合趋向补语 — Compound Complement of Direction

### Lesson 15 — 我想再去一次中国

1. 动量补语（1） — Complement of Frequency (1)
2. 动量补语（2） — Complement of Frequency (2)
3. “有”字句（2） — “有” Sentence (2)
