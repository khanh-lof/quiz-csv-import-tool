# 《新HSK教程2》 New HSK Course 2 — 词语表 / Vocabulary Index

Authoritative word list, transcribed visually from the official textbook's back-matter
词语表 (`Gtrinh HSK 2 - Ms.Ha Chinese.pdf`, PDF pages 157–162; book pp. 141–146).

**How to read this list**

- 课号 = the lesson in which the word is introduced. Two numbers (e.g. `8, 13`) means the word appears in two lessons, usually with different senses.
- **★ = 超纲词, beyond the HSK 3.0 Level-2 syllabus.** These words appear in the book as enrichment. On slides they must be marked optional/mở rộng — never presented as required Level-2 vocabulary.
- Words WITHOUT ★ are the ~200 syllabus words for HSK 3.0 Level 2 covered by this book.
- Homographs are listed separately when the book does (e.g. 过 guò v. vs 过 guo aspect particle).
