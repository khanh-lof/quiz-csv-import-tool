# 《HSK标准教程3》 HSK Standard Course 3 — 词语表 / Vocabulary Index

Authoritative word list, transcribed visually from the textbook's back-matter 词语总表 (PDF pages 195–209, book pp. 194–207).

**How to read this list**

- 课号 = the lesson in which the word is introduced. Several numbers (e.g. `4, 20`) mean the word is printed for several lessons (分, 花, 口, 又).
- **★ = 超纲词, beyond the HSK 3 syllabus** (the book prints `*`, in its own 超纲词 table). 13 words: 词语、各、举行、可乐、秘书、情况、生活、睡着、太太、特点、眼镜、真正、中介. They are placed here in their letter section by pinyin; the book prints them in a separate table.
- Pinyin is exactly as printed, with tone marks (including `bǐjìběn (diànnǎo)`, `chūn (tiān)`, `liáotiān (r)`, `zhǐyǒu……cái……`).
- Words that share characters but are different words (still one row each): 搬/班 bān, 角/脚 jiǎo, 为/位 wèi, 只 zhī (18) / 只 zhǐ (3), 长 zhǎng (16), 还 huán (11) / 还是 háishi (3), 花 huā (noun 3, verb 15, one row).
- The book's table of words made of already-learned characters (旧字新词) is listed after the proper nouns, not in the main tables.
