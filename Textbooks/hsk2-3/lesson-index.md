# 《HSK标准教程3》 HSK Standard Course 3 — Lesson & Language-Point Index

Authoritative index, transcribed visually from the textbook's 目录 / Mục lục
(`HSK Standard Course Level 3.pdf`, PDF pp.8–14). This is the Vietnamese-annotated edition (Nhân Trí Việt / AJT Education):
the book's own text is Chinese with Vietnamese notes beside it; its word list has Vietnamese meanings and lesson numbers.

**Book facts:**

- Level: **HSK 2.0 — Level 3 (三级)**, 北京语言大学出版社 / 汉考国际 (CTI), chief editor 姜丽萍.
- **20 lessons.** Each lesson has a 热身 warm-up, four short texts (课文 1–4, each with its own 生词 box), 注释 (language-point notes), exercises, 语音 / 汉字 / 俗语 pages. Lessons 5, 10, 15 and 20 end with a 文化 (culture) page.
- The word list (词语总表) gives each word with pinyin, Vietnamese POS abbreviation, Vietnamese meaning and 课号. Words printed with `*` in the 生词 boxes and in the contents are **beyond the syllabus** (超纲词, mostly Level 4–5 words, labelled 四级 / 五级 in the book's list). They are marked **★** in the vocabulary index.
- The 生词 boxes give pinyin, a Vietnamese POS abbreviation (`dt.` danh từ = n., `đgt.` động từ = v., `tt.` tính từ = adj., `phó.` = adv., `giới.` = prep., `liên.` = conj., `trợ.` = particle, `lượng.` = measure word, `sl.` = numeral, `đtnn.` = modal verb, `đt.` = pronoun) and a Vietnamese meaning. English glosses are not printed.
- Proper names of the lessons (小丽, 小刚, 小明, 马可, 李静, 大山, 小云, 李小美, 周明 …) are in the 专有名词 section of the word list.

**Page-number mapping:** the book's page numbers equal the PDF page numbers up to lesson 11's first page (book p.106 = PDF p.106). One extra page is inside lesson 11's pages, so from lesson 12 on, **PDF page = book page + 1**. Lesson 1 starts on PDF p.17. The 词语总表 starts on book p.194 = PDF p.195 and runs to PDF p.205 (专有名词 on p.205); PDF pp.206–208 hold the beyond-syllabus list and 补充 words; the exercise answers start on PDF p.209.

## Language points (语法 / Grammar)

### Lesson 1 — 周末你有什么打算？

1. 结果补语“好” — Resultative complement “好”
2. “一……也/都 + 不/没……”表示否定 — Negation with “一……也/都 + 不/没……”
3. 连词“那” — The conjunction “那”

### Lesson 2 — 他什么时候回来？

1. 简单趋向补语 — Simple directional complements
2. 两个动作连续发生 — Two actions happening one after another
3. 反问的表达：能……吗？ — Rhetorical question “能……吗？”

### Lesson 3 — 桌子上放着很多饮料。

1. “还是”和“或者” — “还是” and “或者”
2. 存在的表达：处所词+动词着+数词+量词+名词 — Expressing existence
3. “会”表示可能 — The modal verb “会” for possibility

### Lesson 4 — 她总是笑着跟客人说话。

1. 又……又…… — “又……又……”
2. 动作的伴随：动词₁着（宾语₁）+动词₂（宾语₂） — Accompanying action: V1 着 + V2

### Lesson 5 — 我最近越来越胖了。

1. “了”表示变化 — “了” for change
2. 越来越+形容词/心理动词 — “越来越” + adjective / psychological verb

### Lesson 6 — 怎么突然找不到了？

1. 可能补语：动词+得/不+补语 — Potential complements
2. “呢”询问处所：名词+呢 — “呢” asking about location
3. “刚”和“刚才” — “刚” and “刚才”

### Lesson 7 — 我跟她都认识五年了。

1. 时段的表达 — Expressing a period of time
2. 表达兴趣 — Expressing interest
3. 用“半”“刻”“差”表示时间 — Telling time with “半”, “刻”, “差”

### Lesson 8 — 你去哪儿我就去哪儿。

1. “又”和“再” — “又” and “再”
2. 疑问代词活用1 — Flexible use of interrogative pronouns (1)

### Lesson 9 — 她的汉语说得跟中国人一样好。

1. 越A越B — “越A越B”
2. 比较句1：A跟B一样（+形容词） — Comparison (1): A 跟 B 一样 (+ adjective)

### Lesson 10 — 数学比历史难多了。

1. 比较句2：A比B+形容词+一点儿/一些/得多/多了 — Comparison (2): A 比 B + adjective + degree
2. 概数的表达1 — Expressing approximate numbers (1)

### Lesson 11 — 别忘了把空调关了。

1. “把”字句1：A把B+动词+…… — The 把 sentence (1)
2. 概数的表达2：左右 — Expressing approximate numbers (2): 左右

### Lesson 12 — 把重要的东西放在我这儿吧。

1. “才”和“就” — “才” and “就”
2. “把”字句2：A把B+动词+在/到/给…… — The 把 sentence (2)

### Lesson 13 — 我是走回来的。

1. 复合趋向补语 — Compound directional complements
2. 一边……一边…… — “一边……一边……”

### Lesson 14 — 你把水果拿过来。

1. “把”字句3：A把B+动词+结果补语/趋向补语 — The 把 sentence (3)
2. 先……，再/又……，然后…… — “先……，再/又……，然后……”

### Lesson 15 — 其他都没什么问题。

1. 除了……以外，都/还/也…… — “除了……以外，都/还/也……”
2. 疑问代词活用2 — Flexible use of interrogative pronouns (2)
3. 程度的表达：极了 — Expressing degree: “极了”

### Lesson 16 — 我现在累得下了班就想睡觉。

1. 如果……（的话），（主语）就…… — “如果……（的话），（主语）就……”
2. 复杂的状态补语 — Complex complements of state
3. 单音节形容词重叠 — Reduplication of monosyllabic adjectives

### Lesson 17 — 谁都有办法看好你的“病”。

1. 双音节动词重叠 — Reduplication of disyllabic verbs
2. 疑问代词活用3 — Flexible use of interrogative pronouns (3)

### Lesson 18 — 我相信他们会同意的。

1. 只要……，就…… — “只要……，就……”
2. 介词“关于” — The preposition “关于”

### Lesson 19 — 你没看出来吗？

1. 趋向补语的引申义 — Extended meanings of directional complements
2. “使”“叫”“让” — “使”, “叫” and “让”

### Lesson 20 — 我被他影响了。

1. “被”字句 — The 被 sentence
2. 只有……，才…… — “只有……，才……”
