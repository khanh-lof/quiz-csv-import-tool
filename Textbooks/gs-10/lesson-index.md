## Second source: first-term revision sheet

"GLOBAL SUCCESS 10 – REVISION FOR FIRST TERM" (two images the teacher sent) adds 10 collocations and idioms with
Vietnamese meanings to each of Units 1–5. They are appended to those units' New words as `phrase`, Note
`revision sheet` (or `idiom; revision sheet`); the English glosses are the curator's. The sheet titles Unit 2
"Healthy Lifestyle" while the word list titles it "Humans and the Environment"; its phrases were put in Unit 2 as sent.

## Scan facts

- The PDF has a text layer; no OCR was needed.
- Units 3 and 4 print no part of speech; Unit 1's part-of-speech column is misaligned (e.g. "prepare adj",
  "busy n"). The lesson docs set the right POS and mark the row `POS corrected`.
- Typos repaired in the Word column (the doc's Note keeps the printed form): benefít → benefit, harmíul → harmful,
  refdlable → refillable, Artidcial → Artificial, communit development → community development,
  blending learning → blended learning, "living/ non-living" → living/non-living, "create = form" → create.
- The PDF's Vietnamese font turned some diacritics wrong (ể → ế, ổ → ố, ồ → ô, e.g. "nối tiếng", "biếu diễn",
  "mô côi"); the docs repair them.
- English glosses are the curator's: the source gives Vietnamese meanings only.
